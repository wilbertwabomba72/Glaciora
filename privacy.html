Glaciora Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Glaciora handles information when you use the game. Developer account and contact: hengbozail@163.com.

Information Stored Locally

Glaciora stores gameplay progress, unlocked levels, star ratings, selected hero, and audio settings locally on your device. This local gameplay data is used to keep your progress and preferences available between sessions.

Advertising And App Tracking Transparency

Glaciora may request App Tracking Transparency permission on supported iOS versions. If permission is requested, iOS will show Apple's system prompt and you can allow or deny tracking. Advertising SDKs may use identifiers or ad-related signals according to your consent, iOS settings, and their own SDK behavior.

Third-Party SDKs

The app includes third-party advertising SDKs and a security SDK. These SDKs may process device identifiers, advertising data, security signals, network information, or related technical data to provide ads, prevent abuse, measure ad delivery, and support SDK security functions.

Network Use

Core gameplay is designed for local single-player play. The app may still use network connections for advertising configuration, ad loading, ad measurement, and security SDK functions.

Children's Privacy

Glaciora does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact the developer so the concern can be reviewed.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact: hengbozail@163.com.
