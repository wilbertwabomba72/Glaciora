About Glaciora

Glaciora is a single-player card-battle game built for quick offline sessions. Across 50 levels, players choose a hero, manage cards and energy, study enemy intent, and unlock progress locally on their device.

The game supports iPhone and iPad, uses SpriteKit and Objective-C, and runs on iOS 13 and later.

Read The Duel

Each enemy shows intent before acting, giving every turn a clear tactical choice between attack, defense, healing, and setup.

Choose Your Hero

Play as a shield-focused knight, a spell-driven mage, or a faster ranger with extra energy pressure.

Climb The Realms

Win battles, earn stars from remaining HP, unlock new levels, and face stronger boss fights every 10 stages.
